Ice Cream Tycoon

Run your own ice cream stand! Start with Chocolate and Vanilla, sell scoops to customers, and buy rarer ice cream at the shop for more money.

🛒 Ice Cream Shop

Buy a tub, then hold it and place it on your stand. Rarer ice cream takes longer to grow but sells for more. The stock changes every 3 minutes.

👕 Avatar Shop

Pick how your character looks. Everyone in the park will see it!

⚡ Fuse Machine

Pick 2 ice creams to fuse into 1 new ice cream worth more than both! Some pairs make secret special ice creams.
